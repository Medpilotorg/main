Honest guidance for future doctors

MedPilot exists to make studying medicine in Bulgaria simple, transparent and stress-free, with genuine long-term support for every student.

Founded on a simple frustration

Too many agencies promise the world, take their fee at admission and disappear. Students arrive in a new country with no support, no community and nobody to call.

MedPilot was founded by Kirstie Mensah and Sharon Egeonu to do the opposite: give students honest advice about where they can realistically get in, charge clearly for exactly what we do, and stay by their side throughout their entire degree. Our student ambassadors are studying in Bulgaria right now, and our alumni network keeps growing with every graduating class.

  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees, ever
  • A personal case manager for every student
  • Support that continues for the whole degree

What we stand for

Mission

Simple, transparent, stress-free

To make studying medicine in Bulgaria simple, transparent and stress-free, while providing genuine long-term support to every student.

Vision

The most trusted name

To become the most trusted international medical admissions agency for Bulgaria, and eventually expand into other European countries.

Values

Honesty before commission

Honest advice, clear pricing and real accountability. If a university isn't right for you, we say so, even when it costs us.
