Applying abroad is complicated.
It shouldn't be confusing.

Complicated admissions, visa hurdles, unfamiliar universities and unreliable information stop capable students from becoming doctors. We remove every one of those barriers.

Honest admission advice

We tell you which universities genuinely fit your grades and goals, not which ones pay the highest commission.

End-to-end paperwork

Document preparation, translations, entrance exam registration and visa applications, handled with you at every stage.

One case manager, always

You get a named person who knows your file, answers your questions and stays with you from enquiry to enrolment.

Most agencies stop at admission. We stay for the whole degree.

Getting in is only the beginning. MedPilot keeps supporting you throughout your studies with academic guidance, welfare support and a community of students and alumni who have made the same journey.

  • Academic guidance from current medical student ambassadors
  • Welfare check-ins and an emergency support line
  • An online community of students living in Bulgaria right now
  • Practical help with everyday life, from housing to residence permits
