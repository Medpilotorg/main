Your journey, in ten legs

Every student follows a clear route. You always know exactly where you are and what comes next.

  1. 01
    Departure

    Free consultation

    Tell us about your grades, goals and budget. We give you an honest picture of your options.

  2. 02
    Check-in

    Eligibility check

    We assess your qualifications against each university's entry requirements.

  3. 03
    Route selection

    Choose your university

    Compare accredited medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary schools with our guidance.

  4. 04
    Documents

    Prepare and submit documents

    We check, translate and legalise everything so nothing delays your application.

  5. 05
    Application

    Application submitted

    Your case manager files your application and tracks it with the university directly.

  1. 06
    Exam

    Entrance exam

    Guidance and preparation support for your biology and chemistry entrance exams.

  2. 07
    Cleared for boarding

    Offer letter

    Your official acceptance arrives. We talk you through enrolment and fees.

  3. 08
    Visa

    Travel visa

    Full support with your D visa application, appointments and paperwork.

  4. 09
    Arrival

    Arrival support

    Airport pickup, accommodation, bank account, SIM card and local orientation.

  5. 10
    Cruising altitude

    Ongoing support

    Residence permit help, mentoring and welfare support throughout your degree.
